## New in 0.7.17

- **The last Topic Modelling step is much faster**: "finding each topic's words" could run for minutes on one processor with a large corpus. It now takes seconds and uses all the processors it may. The topics, their words and the map are exactly as before.
- **Topic Modelling Display options, beside the bubble chart on wide screens**: the Result settings are now called **Display options**, because they change how the result is shown. When the results panel is wide, they sit beside a 4:3 bubble chart, one option per row with the colour legend, instead of below a wide chart. Drag the divider between them to change the chart's width (double-click to return to 4:3), or click the panel icon at the right of the heading to move the options below the chart, where they take a compact row with each label beside its control. Click **Display options** to fold them to one summary line on a small screen. **Find topics** is now **Search term**, and **Words** is **Words per topic**. The tab remembers these choices on this computer.
