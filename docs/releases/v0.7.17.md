## New in 0.7.17

- **The last Topic Modelling step is much faster**: "finding each topic's words" could run for minutes on one processor with a large corpus. It now takes seconds and uses all the processors it may. The topics, their words and the map are exactly as before.
- **More compact Result settings in Topic Modelling**: each label sits beside its control, so the settings take about half the height. On a small screen, click **Result settings** to fold them to one summary line; **Find topics** and **Add to Project** stay available, and the tab remembers the choice on this computer.
- **Topic Modelling settings beside the bubble chart on wide screens**: when the results panel is wide, the Result settings and the colour legend sit beside a 4:3 bubble chart instead of below a wide one. Drag the divider between them to change the chart's width (double-click to return to 4:3), or click the button after **Add to Project** to move the settings below the chart. The tab remembers these choices on this computer.
