## New in 0.7.17

- **The last Topic Modelling step is much faster**: "finding each topic's words" could run for minutes on one processor with a large corpus. It now takes seconds and uses all the processors it may. The topics, their words and the map are exactly as before.
- **Topic Modelling Display options, beside the bubble chart on wide screens**: the Result settings are now called **Display options**, because they change how the result is shown. When the results panel is wide, they sit beside a 4:3 bubble chart, one option per row with the colour legend, instead of below a wide chart. Drag the divider between them to change the chart's width (double-click to return to 4:3), or click the panel icon at the right of the heading to move the options below the chart, where they take a compact row with each label beside its control. Click **Display options** to fold them to one summary line on a small screen. **Find topics** is now **Search term**, **Per document** is **Topics per doc**, and **Words** is **Words per topic**. The Topics slider shows its smallest and largest values under the bar. The tab remembers these choices on this computer.
- **Search several terms at once in Topic Modelling**: the **Search term** now takes several words separated by spaces, commas or other punctuation, and finds topics with any of them. Each term's matching words are bold in their own colour in the topic list and in a bubble's word cloud, so with "chil, home" *child* and *children* share one colour and *home* and *homeless* another. The box shows an example: "e.g. house, pre, gov".
- **The Topics slider starts at two topics** for new runs, because a single topic is the whole corpus. Results from earlier runs keep their range.
