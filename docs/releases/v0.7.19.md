## New in 0.7.19

- **Tasks stays tidy**: a successful import or upload now leaves the **Tasks** panel on its own 10 minutes after it finishes. Failed and cancelled ones stay until you clear them, so you don't miss an error. The new brush button beside the **Tasks** heading clears all finished imports and uploads at once. Finished imports are also cleared when you start the desktop app again, or when you sign out of a shared server. Your imported files are never removed.
- **Check for Updates always shows the newest version (desktop app)**: if the update window from an earlier check was still open, **Check for Updates** could offer an older version than the newest. It now checks again. This fix takes effect from the version after the one you install it with.
